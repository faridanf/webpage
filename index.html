I work in algebraic combinatorics, studying the structures that connect Hopf algebras, symmetric functions, and representation theory. I also explore how computational and machine learning methods can help discover patterns in mathematics and formulate useful conjectures. I also study AI safety, especially interpretability, jailbreaks, and adversarial attacks.

About

FA

A mathematician searching for mathematical meaning and AI safety.

I am a Professor of Mathematics at the Research Center for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Shandong University. Before joining Shandong University, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of British Columbia and a Visiting Assistant Professor at the University of Colorado Boulder. I received my PhD in mathematics from York University.
